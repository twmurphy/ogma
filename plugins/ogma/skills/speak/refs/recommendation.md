# Recommendation

Reference for [`speak`](../SKILL.md).

Use this when you are proposing an approach and the user has a real choice to make. Focus your response around one decision -- if other decisions must be made, they should made separately. The recommendation comes last. 

Say what you would do, and optionally provide viable alternatives and meaningful trade-offs. 

Use the `AskUserQuestion' tool, with the recommended choice highlighted first.

---

## Lay out in two sections

```markdown

---
**My <strong/soft/tentative/etc> recommendation**

<Recommendation>
<Tradeoffs>

---
**<Worth considering/Rejected/Equally viable routes/etc>**

<Alternative 1>
<Tradeoffs>

<Alternative 2>
<Tradeoffs>

```
